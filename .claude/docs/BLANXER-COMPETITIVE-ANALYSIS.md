## 🎯 BLANXER FEATURES ANALYSIS

### 💳 PAYMENT FEATURES (Blanxer Has)

#### 1. Blanxer Payment Fulfillment Service
- **Fonepay Terminal Integration** (exclusive to Blanxer clients)
- **Transaction Fee**: 2.75% - 3.9% per transaction
- **KYC Verification Required** for activation
- **QR Payment Support** (all wallets and mobile banking)

#### 2. Self-Purchased Payment Services
- **Nepal Payment Solution** integration
- **Nabil Bank** (Visa/Mastercard)
- **eSewa Merchant Integration** (merchant code + secret key)
- **Custom payment method configuration**

#### 3. QR Payment System
- **Universal QR Code** (supports all wallets)
- **Mobile banking integration**
- **Multiple payment options** in single QR

### 🏢 STORE SETTINGS & COMPLIANCE

#### 1. Store Details
- **Store Name** (required)
- **Business Category** (dropdown selection)
- **Contact Number** (required)
- **Store Address** (required)
- **Contact Email** (required)

#### 2. E-commerce Compliance Information
- **Registered Business Name**
- **PAN/VAT Number**
- **Company Address**
- **Registration Number**
- **E-commerce Number**
- **Complaint Officer** details
- **Outlets** management

#### 3. KYC Verification System
- **Business Registration Information**
  - Registered Business Name

- **Document Upload System**
