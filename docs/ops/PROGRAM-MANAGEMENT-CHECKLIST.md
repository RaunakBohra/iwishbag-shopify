# 🗂️ Program Management Checklist

> Operational tasks to keep delivery aligned with the roadmap and ensure smooth launches.

## 1. Backlog & Ticketing
- [ ] Break each checklist item into tracker tickets with owners, estimates, and due dates.
- [ ] Maintain single source of truth (Linear/Jira) with labels for platform areas.
- [ ] Review backlog grooming weekly; archive stale items >90 days old.

## 2. Roadmap & Reporting
- [ ] Publish 24-sprint roadmap slide (rolling 6 months) and update monthly.
- [ ] Track sprint burndown charts; include in weekly status email.
- [ ] Maintain risk log (technical/product) with mitigation owners.

## 3. Reviews & Ceremonies
- [ ] Schedule weekly architecture, security, and testing review meetings.
- [ ] Record notes + action items; circulate to stakeholders within 24h.
- [ ] Hold monthly platform demo for stakeholders (recording stored in shared drive).

## 4. Metrics & KPIs
- [ ] Define KPI dashboard (merchants, ARR, churn, NPS) and data sources.
- [ ] Automate dashboard refresh cadence (weekly) and share snapshot.
- [ ] Establish alert thresholds (e.g., churn > 2% per month triggers review).

## 5. Launch & Support
- [ ] Draft post-launch support rota (+24h coverage) and share with CS team.
- [ ] Prepare customer success onboarding materials (playbooks, FAQ).
- [ ] Set up feedback intake (Intercom/Zendesk) with escalation paths.

## 6. Documentation & Communication
- [ ] Keep roadmap, decisions, and retros in shared knowledge base (Notion/Confluence).
- [ ] Summarize weekly progress (wins, blockers, next focus) for leadership.
- [ ] Maintain change log for major product/process decisions.
